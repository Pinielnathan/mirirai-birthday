# For Mirirai

A late birthday wish for Mirirai's 21st (28 September 2005 → 28 September 2026).

A single self-contained page (`index.html`): envelope opening, a rewind back to 28 September, the night she was born (moon, Libra, sapphire, aster), live counters of her trips around the Sun, 21 wishes hidden in the stars, a cake with 21 candles you can blow out through the microphone, and a letter.

Add `#skip` to the URL to jump straight past the opening.
